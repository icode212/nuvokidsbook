# Nuvo
**Nuvo is a curious five-year-old who finds big adventures in everyday places.** A puddle becomes a lake to explore, a garden becomes a tiny jungle, and a cardboard box becomes a spaceship.

For broad international appeal, give him a clear personality and a simple, recognizable appearance. No character will appeal to everyone, but curiosity, kindness, and playful mistakes can make Nuvo relatable across cultures.

**Character appearance**

- **Face:** Round cheeks, a small rounded nose, dark brown eyes, and expressive eyebrows.
- **Skin:** Warm medium-brown.
- **Hair:** Short, softly wavy dark brown hair with one recognizable curl at the front.
- **Build:** Childlike proportions, small hands, and a slightly oversized head for a friendly picture-book appearance.
- **Clothes:** A teal T-shirt, mustard-yellow overalls with a large front pocket, and simple cream sneakers.
- **Adventure accessory:** A small coral-red backpack containing a notebook, crayons, and a magnifying glass.

Keep his everyday outfit free of national flags, slogans, and religious symbols. His appearance can stay consistent while different stories introduce authentic places, clothing, languages, and friends.

**Personality**

Nuvo is enthusiastic and observant. He asks **“I wonder…”**, notices little things, and sometimes guesses incorrectly. He learns by looking, listening, and trying again.

He is kind to animals, asks before touching unfamiliar things, and accepts help. He can feel nervous, tired, or disappointed—he does not have to be brave or cheerful all the time. His adventures involve cooperation and discovery rather than fighting or winning.

**His signature gesture:** He tilts his head, raises one eyebrow, and opens his notebook when something surprises him.

**His recurring phrase:** “What will we discover?”

**First scenes for your books**

| Scene | Illustration idea | Interactive discoveries |
|---|---|---|
| **Nuvo at the Farm** | Nuvo stands beside a wooden fence, watching a cow and calf | Tap animals for movement, names, and sounds |
| **Nuvo’s Garden Adventure** | Nuvo kneels beside a flower bed with his magnifying glass | Discover a butterfly, snail, leaves, and watering can |
| **Nuvo and the Rainy Day** | Nuvo wears rain boots and a yellow raincoat beside a trusted adult | Tap rain, an umbrella, puddles, and dripping leaves |
| **Nuvo’s Cardboard Spaceship** | Nuvo sits in a homemade cardboard rocket in his playroom | Tap pretend controls, a star mobile, and toy planets |

For the app, illustrate **Nuvo, the background, and each interactive object as separate layers**. Keep Nuvo near the edge of a scene so children can clearly see and tap the discoveries.

# Milo, Lina, Pip

Give Nuvo **two friends with different ways of exploring**, plus a pet who brings gentle humour. Keep them all around age five, with equally important roles.

| Character | Appearance | Personality | Recognizable detail |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Milo — the thoughtful builder** | Light warm skin, straight chestnut-brown hair with a sideways fringe, brown eyes, and round blue glasses. Coral-orange T-shirt, navy shorts, cream sneakers. | Likes puzzles, patterns, and making things. Usually pauses to look before trying. Sometimes his careful plans need a little improvisation. | A small blue pouch containing a notebook and crayons. |
| **Lina — the imaginative explorer** | Deep brown skin, dark brown eyes, and tightly curled black hair in two rounded puffs. Lavender T-shirt, sage-green overalls, cream sneakers. | Invents stories, notices sounds, and loves discovering how things work. Confident about sharing ideas, but learns to listen to other suggestions. | A sunny-yellow backpack with a simple flower-shaped patch. |
| **Pip — the curious puppy** | Small cream-and-caramel dog with floppy ears, one caramel patch around an eye, short legs, and a curled tail. Plain teal harness. | Affectionate, playful, and easily distracted by leaves. Often notices something the children have missed. | One ear lifts slightly whenever Pip hears an interesting sound. |

**How they work together**

Nuvo asks, **“What will we discover?”**  
Milo says, **“Let’s look closely.”**  
Lina suggests, **“What if we try this?”**  
Pip tilts his head—and follows a fluttering leaf.

Let these be tendencies rather than fixed jobs. Lina can solve the puzzle, Milo can suggest the adventure, and Nuvo can need reassurance. All three can lead, make mistakes, and help each other.

**Their first shared scene**

The children discover a snail beside a garden path. Nuvo crouches to watch it, Milo sketches its spiral shell, and Lina wonders where it is going. Pip starts to sniff, then sits beside Nuvo while they give the snail space.

For consistent illustrations, keep the same **rounded picture-book style, childlike proportions, warm painted texture, and simple clothing** as Nuvo. Give each child a distinct silhouette: **Nuvo’s front curl, Milo’s glasses and fringe, Lina’s two hair puffs**, and Pip’s floppy ears.

These are working names; check their meanings and pronunciation in your main launch languages before finalizing them.

# Mimi
Meet Mimi, Lina’s curious kitten and Pip’s best friend.

She has soft grey fur, a cream chest, white paws, green eyes, and a lavender collar. Mimi watches carefully before exploring, loves chasing leaves, and often curls up beside Pip after an adventure.

# Little Nuvo

**Little Nuvo is Nuvo’s favourite plush toy—a cuddly purple companion who comes along on everyday adventures.** Nuvo loved his toy so much that he gave it his own name. His friends call the toy “Little Nuvo” to tell them apart.

Little Nuvo has a soft, round purple body, a small tuft on top, big white eyes, a friendly smile, short floppy arms, and rounded feet. **The yellow star on his tummy is his signature feature.**

In the story world, Little Nuvo is a comforting toy. He peeks out of Nuvo’s coral backpack, sits beside him during drawing time, and gets a cuddle when something feels unfamiliar. Through Nuvo’s imagination, he becomes a curious, playful friend who celebrates discoveries and reminds everyone that learning takes practice.

In **Nuvo ABC**, Little Nuvo comes to life as the child’s encouraging learning companion. He celebrates effort, responds gently to mistakes, and invites children to try again.

His personality is **warm, patient, curious, and a little silly**. He never teases or pressures children.

His favourite phrase:

**“Little steps, big discoveries!”**
