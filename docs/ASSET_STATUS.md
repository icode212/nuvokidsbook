## Development behavior, clearly disclosed in the parent area

- Static scene mode uses hit areas with a gentle highlight. It does **not** animate the illustrated animal or move the flattened image as if it were a separate animal layer.
- Farm version 0.2.0 uses 74 prepared Samantha voice files generated locally with macOS `say` at 145 words/minute, matching NewNuvoKids. All story, identification, Find it, success and hint lines have a file. They are cancellable, user-initiated and included in the verified offline pack. This is synthesized preview narration; final voice performance and commercial-rights review remain open.
- Discovery sound feedback uses a quiet synthesized tone. It does **not** reproduce a moo, baa, quack or other animal sound. No third-party audio has been added or represented as licensed.
- Reduced-motion preference and parent motion controls suppress highlight animation.

## Reusable art brief

Use exact character reference files. Warm textured gouache, rounded shapes, cream/teal/amber palette, gentle expressions, consistent five-year-old proportions. Nuvo wears teal/mustard with coral backpack; Milo has blue glasses, coral shirt and navy shorts; Lina has two puffs with lavender ties, lavender shirt, sage overalls and yellow backpack. Pip is cream/caramel with teal harness; Mimi is silver-grey with white paws and a short cream-tipped tail.

Keep established farm composition and visitor barriers. Export background without interactive subjects or embedded lettering, repair removed-subject areas, and export each subject with alpha and a clean silhouette. Author exact normalized scene placement and hit geometry. Review anatomy, accidental text, scale, perspective and safety before acceptance. Never replace approved art merely to obtain a stylistic variation.
